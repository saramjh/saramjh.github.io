This page explains the third-party services used by **DevTestudinidae** (saramjh.github.io) and the information those services may process when you use the root blog.

This policy applies to the **root DevTestudinidae blog**. Some paths on the same GitHub Pages hostname are separately maintained project sites and may use different services or policies.

## Services used on this blog

### Google AdSense

This blog uses **Google AdSense** to serve advertising on eligible production pages. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, IP-derived location, browser/device information, ad interaction data, and similar information to deliver, measure, and protect advertising.

AdSense is disabled on pages that explicitly opt out of advertising, including this Privacy Policy and the 404 page.

Google's information about how it uses data from sites and apps that use its services is available at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Google Analytics

This blog uses **Google Analytics 4** (G-4DYFKSNFBG) to understand traffic and usage patterns such as pages viewed, referral source, device category, and engagement. Analytics data is used to evaluate site performance and improve content.

Google's privacy information is available at [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Microsoft Clarity

This blog uses **Microsoft Clarity** to understand how visitors interact with pages, including aggregated interaction and session-behavior information. This is used to identify usability and performance problems.

Microsoft's privacy statement is available at [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

### Disqus comments

Pages that enable comments use **Disqus**. If you load or use the comment area, Disqus may process information according to its own privacy practices. Comments are not required to read the blog.

Disqus privacy information is available at [Disqus Privacy Policy](https://disqus.com/privacy-policy/).

## Local browser storage

The site stores the selected light/dark theme in your browser's local storage so that the preference can be restored on later visits.

Third-party services listed above may use cookies or other browser storage according to their own settings and policies.

## Advertising and consent choices

For visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Google's Privacy & messaging system provides advertising consent choices, including **Consent**, **Do not consent**, and **Manage options**. The message appears where the regional rules apply, on pages that load AdSense.

To change a previous advertising-consent decision, use **privacy choices** in the footer of an advertising-enabled blog post. This link appears when Google's consent API confirms that European regulations apply to your visit. This policy page itself does not load AdSense or analytics scripts.

You can also manage or delete cookies and site storage using your browser settings. Blocking or deleting storage may affect analytics, advertising, comments, or saved site preferences.
