I am **DevTestudinidae**, an independent developer. I build public-facing software and document the design decisions, fixes and trade-offs that come with maintaining it.

My work includes [Spoonie](/portfolio/spoonie/), a cooking-recipe product; [KoreAves](/portfolio/koreaves/), an English–Korean atlas of bird information in South Korea; and [Space Atlas](/portfolio/space-atlas/), interactive space-science learning materials. The [project portfolio](/portfolio/) links to their live work and explains what has actually been built.

I also write about AI-assisted development, automation, browser tooling and troubleshooting. Posts include reproducible steps and measurements when I have them. I keep work in progress and unverified outcomes distinct from released features.

For independent records, see the [external publications and listings archive](/portfolio/recognition/). It separates credited writing, directory listings and catalogue entries. My [10,000 Birds guest article](https://www.10000birds.com/the-bird-photo-i-could-use-was-not-the-bird-photo-i-wanted.htm) appears under the byline Jihun Oh; [One Earth published a KoreAves feature](https://www.oneearth.org/koreaves/). These records document publication and inclusion; they do not measure readership or product adoption.

Source code and public projects: [github.com/saramjh](https://github.com/saramjh).
